The packet is Internal C3 and belongs in the builder's source inputs; it must not be swept automatically into a static build's downloadable assets.

## 2. Detailed image analysis

### What to carry forward

The deep green application header establishes continuity without dominating the canvas. The warm, pale reading surface and generous space around the principal composition give an editorial rather than dashboard tone. Typographic hierarchy is much stronger than a grid of equal status cards. The selective stacked illustration provides grouping and depth; its quieter surrounding objects suggest that the system contains more than the model itself.

The source/evidence specimen at the lower right is especially useful as a direction: it makes an abstract topic inspectable. The right-side context area suggests how relevant provenance, uncertainty and related objects could remain close to a selection. The consistent icon scale and restrained lines could provide a learnable graphic vocabulary if each symbol has a distinct purpose.

Carry forward those qualities as principles, not exact assets. Use the approved SEB Sans Serif when available and an honest fallback when it is not. Do not copy the image's serif face, emblem, text or generated enterprise-document snippet as if they were approved assets or facts. Keep large photographs and faces out of the explanation.

### What still obstructs understanding

Some arrows in the central composition are difficult to assign to a proposition. A reader should not have to infer whether “obligations and intent” come from policies or flow back into them. Name the relationship and make its source/target evident. Short words such as informs, constrains, produces and verifies carry more meaning than an unlabeled connector.

The image is a strong mood and proportion reference. It is not evidence that this composition is navigable or that its implied system exists.

## 3. Recommended opening and selection behaviour

Use the current plan's broad work-centred anchor with the image's calmer hierarchy and selective depth. One canonical relationship model supports several lenses; the reader does not enter separate narratives for layers, enablers and evidence.

**Heading:** How AI fits into CLM work

**Primary action:** See how context supports a CLM task

The first route opens the source-of-wealth preparation specimen. The task name is recognizable before the click. Kaicy's named implementation is available within its deeper, dated explanation. Optional interests remain requirements/change, data/access/operation and topic/source search; do not add the image's second row of six global actions.

### What the graphic explains before a click

Show useful CLM work and its output; a selected artifact considered for reuse; the separate reuse-scope review; and further work informed by it. Preserve immediate development value even when nothing is reused. Display information routes and operating support where the selected task needs them. The authority boundary around customer-record change remains explicit.

The review labels should state purpose: checking the present output and establishing scope for another task. They do not claim a named reuse owner already exists. The packet says that owner is unresolved. Human accountability can be visible without pretending every deterministic step requires an additional manual click.

### What a selection changes

Selecting the source reveals the actual synthetic passage and its evidence pointer. Selecting context reveals field meaning, subject scope and the relevant source version. Selecting the method shows how observations are prepared and what is deliberately left unresolved. Selecting a condition changes the available route and resulting output. Selecting evidence shows the recorded version/scope and whether the primary source is supplied or only reported.

These are additions to understanding, not caption changes. The broad anchor and current task remain recognizable. On wide screens, an object inspector can help; it need not be permanently filled with generic cards. Long explanations use normal reading space or a native route with reliable return. On mobile, ordered specimens with explicit links replace the desktop perspective stack.

Use one strong selection treatment and conventional focus behaviour. Essential meaning remains visible without hover or motion. Depth groups material; it does not encode approval, benefit or maturity. Every precise label and control remains accessible rather than baked into a bitmap.

### Composition to prototype first

The image puts an abstract layer stack at the centre. The proposed opening instead gives that visual weight to a small collection of recognizable work fragments: a requirement with acceptance logic, a journey/field excerpt, and an instruction with a version. A short annotation explains what each contributes. This keeps the reader close to CLM work while retaining the image's authored depth and proportion.

Arrange the relationship so that three contributions are distinguishable without presenting them as consecutive maturity stages. First, AI can help produce a useful output for today's task. Second, a selected artifact may become context for another task after its reuse scope is established. Third, appropriate data and operating arrangements may extend that assistance into a shared workflow. The first contribution does not wait for the third, and not every output enters the second.

The further task should be specific enough to interpret: preparing source-of-wealth observations, developing a FenX behaviour or analysing a change. Keep only the active route visually strong. Other routes are recognizable alternatives, not a web of equally prominent arrows. The preparation route makes source passages and field meanings inspectable; the development route makes expected behaviour and tests inspectable. A requirement does not become evidence simply because it is drawn next to a test.

Place a brief review annotation at the transfer between tasks, with the task's own review beside its output. Attach a missing-condition consequence to the affected route: without a live interface, supplied-document preparation remains possible but live retrieval does not. Present operating support alongside the work it enables rather than underneath everything as a universal platform foundation. Golden-record change has its own visible authority boundary.

This is the first composition to prototype, not a fixed three-column template. On mobile, use a reading sequence of work fragment, contribution, review boundary and next-task consequence. Preserve the same object identities and selections. On desktop, use overlap sparingly so no label, control or source reference is hidden by perspective.

| Reading state | Dominant material | Depth and return |
|---|---|---|
| Before selection | Broad CLM relationship, recognizable artifacts and one explicit starting task | Existing topics remain discoverable through shared navigation/search |
| Task selected | Source/output or requirement/test pair; relevant context and a visible missing condition | Show the substantial existing explanation in the same task context; optional object inspection |
| Detailed explanation | Contents, method, applicability, responsibility and failure modes | Full tables and useful tabs remain available; avoid compressing them into inspector captions |
| Source or historical evidence | Actual text, source/version, scope and recorded limitations | Return restores task, selected object, expansion state, focus and reading position |

A source link proves traceability to a record, not the record's applicability or the output's correctness. Avoid a single green badge that collapses authority, review, deployment and evidence strength. Name the particular status that matters in the selected view. Any decoration that competes with specimen legibility should be removed during rendered inspection.

## 5. A concrete specimen with honest field and output boundaries

### Input and subject

Use an invented, indexed passage such as:

> CLIENT [14]: I sold my company in 2024.

The subject is a synthetic natural-person client. No sale amount, net worth, annual income, corroboration, verification outcome or plausibility conclusion is inferred. A statement about a business sale identifies a possible wealth-origin event; it does not prove the sale proceeds, the individual's wealth or an approved lookup value.

The packet reports that the journey reference distinguishes `individualSourceOfWealth` from related-party `SourceOfWealth` and legal-entity `legalEntitySourceOfWealth`. Make that difference inspectable. Do not use the LE policy definition as proof of NP policy applicability merely because the economic meaning is similar. The packet's “fits the policy exactly” statement is too strong for NP: its own unresolved list says that NP text still needs verification.

### Field identity and versions

A field key alone is insufficient. Keep entity type, target subject and relevant party/group index, together with policy/reference version and any category discriminator needed to resolve the source. This is a working identity to verify, not an invented enterprise identifier.

The August journey reference's twelve-field table and the later policy export's four analyst-judgement keys are separate source views. Do not treat names such as `sowVerificationStatus`, `plausibilityStatement`, `howWasSourceOfWealthVerified` and `sourceOfFundsAndWealthPlausibilityStatement` as aliases without a verified mapping. Status, verification method and plausibility have different meanings even when they concern the same review.

At the mechanism depth, show the relevant field meaning and subject scope. The practitioner depth can show the reported full field table with version and source-chain qualification. Avoid loading all policy-row counts or every field into the opening.

### Output and explanation are different records

The packet reports an extraction output restricted to five keys: `field_key`, `value`, `evidence_line_ids`, `confidence_level` and `notes`. The server reconstructs evidence from indexed lines. The report describes a deterministic validator whose invalid items remain saved with an INVALID flag. A schema-shaped item is not necessarily valid, verified or fit to use.

The Hub can illustrate a candidate event description for `sourceOfWealthSignificantEvent`, linked to line 14. The current NP wealth-origin lookup list is not supplied. Keep its categorical candidate unresolved; do not invent a “business sale” lookup code or fill net worth from it. Exact confidence-value conventions and required-field rules are also not supplied, so do not claim a mock JSON object passed the real schema.

Maintain separate records:

| Record | Contains | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Synthetic source | Speaker, passage, indexed lines and explicit missing information | Invented input, not a customer transcript |
| Illustrative extraction proposal | Field/subject reference, candidate event text, evidence pointer and declared unresolved categorisation | Based on a reported mechanism, not a tested current response |
| Hub explanation/review annotation | Missing information, applicability, responsibility, reuse scope and provenance | Additional demonstrator metadata; not fields the reported extraction schema emits |
| Task review | Source fidelity, correct subject/field, unresolved values and next information needs | Does not assess customer risk or update FenX |
| Reuse review candidate | What knowledge/method may be maintained for a different purpose, with ownership/permission questions | No assigned reuse authority or automatic promotion |

Knowledge-package consumption also needs precision. The packet reports policy fields supplied to the model and no broader `{knowledge}` prompt slot. That is not “no context”; it is a specific context path. Adding curated documents elsewhere does not prove the runtime consumes them. A future assistant needs a defined, tested consumption route for the additional knowledge and methods.

## 6. Human responsibility: keep the three different questions clear

The reported analyst-field guard issue is useful as an anonymised mechanism: category membership alone does not establish that an AI may populate every field. A reference can contain candidate factual observations, verification status and judgement in the same section.

This supports a field-level boundary between preparation and analyst judgement, and the need for a controlled record-update route. It does **not by itself prove** the separate review needed for reuse. Reuse review has its own rationale: the next task may have different purpose, subject, source/version, authority, maintenance and consumption conditions.

The demonstration must therefore make three questions understandable without claiming three universal manual gates:

1. Is this output suitable for the present task?
2. Which record changes/actions are actually permitted through the applicable process?
3. Which selected artifact can inform another task within an established reuse scope?

The demonstrator holds verification/plausibility conclusions for human judgement. Label this as its intended boundary. Do not claim the named implementation already enforces a code-level guard: the packet reports otherwise, and I have not inspected that repository. Keep named source/code concerns in qualified practitioner reading, not as a defect headline in the opening.

Functional requirements described analyst selection and a tactical manual-entry path. Current API integration and production status are unresolved. The Hub's example performs neither selection into a live system nor a write.

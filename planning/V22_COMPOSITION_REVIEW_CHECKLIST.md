# V22 Composition Review Checklist

Use this after each 1920×1200 render.

## Visual

- One dominant visual idea is obvious.
- The primary reasoning operation is visible before reading long prose.
- The 1920-wide canvas is used for simultaneous context, not only larger margins.
- The scene is not reducible to generic cards + icons.
- Geometry materially improves understanding.
- Material differences correspond to semantic differences.
- Negative space creates hierarchy rather than emptiness.
- Interaction, if present, reveals meaning.
- The main visual would be missed if removed.

## Semantic

- Source-linked != verified.
- Reviewed != approved.
- Structured != applicable != executable.
- Proposed != implemented.
- Designed test != observed pass.
- Prior-version evidence remains pinned to its version/scope.
- Human judgement/authority is visible where it acts.
- Missing/uncertain information remains visible.
- Relationship lines have explicit meaning.
- No visual property silently implies readiness, maturity or approval.

## Comparison

- Active reference captures are visible beside the render.
- Each reference has a `learn_this` and `do_not_copy` tag.
- Largest remaining visual gap is named.
- Largest semantic risk is named.
- At least one competing direction is rejected before nomination.
- The selected direction is revised once after critique before acceptance.

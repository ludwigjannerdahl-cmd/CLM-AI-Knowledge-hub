# V22 Reference Research Notes

Research date: 2026-10-15

Purpose: record why the selected public references are useful to the composition-first sprint. This is research input, not CLM factual authority.

## Research libraries

### Land-book

Public gallery with filters across industry, style, type and typography. Useful because references can be selected intentionally rather than by general aesthetic similarity.

Application to V22: search for a small number of macro-composition examples matching editorial / finance-tech / illustration / visible-border / big-type characteristics. Do not use commercial CTA structure as Hub IA.

Source: https://land-book.com/

### Lapa Ninja

Curates thousands of landing pages plus full-page screenshots and recordings. Its value for V22 is page rhythm: how visual intensity changes across a long page rather than how one hero looks.

Application to V22: use after golden-scene selection to study pacing between expressive and dense modes.

Source: https://www.lapa.ninja/

### Mobbin

Large library of shipped app and web screens, flows, animations and UI elements.

Application to V22: source/detail/search/evidence/history/inspector ergonomics after the golden scenes; not the global art direction.

Source: https://mobbin.com/

### SaaSFrame

Combines websites, products, flows, filtering and responsive variants.

Application to V22: bridge between authored web composition and analytical software workspace patterns.

Source: https://www.saasframe.io/

### Page Flows

Focuses on real-world user flows, recordings and step-by-step interaction examples.

Application to V22: interaction-state research for selection, filtering, drill-down, review and transitions. Do not use as global style reference.

Source: https://pageflows.com/screens/desktop/saas/

### SiteInspire

Curated high-end site gallery with strong coverage of typographic, art-direction, minimal, grid and unusual-layout work.

Application to V22: secondary macro-composition research.

Source: https://www.siteinspire.com/

### One Page Love

Curated one-page sites, templates and section examples.

Application to V22: long-scroll narrative economy and section pacing; not analytical workspace design.

Sources:
- https://onepagelove.com/inspiration
- https://onepagelove.com/sections

### Awwwards

Large catalogue of interaction design, illustration, scroll, WebGL and experimental techniques.

Application to V22: borrow one purposeful interaction technique at a time only after the static reasoning representation is already strong.

Source: https://www.awwwards.com/websites/interaction-design/

## Direct references

### Initiate Architecture

The homepage uses the viewport as a spatial canvas, not a conventional card grid, and explicitly invites mouse-based exploration of transformation.

Application to V22: strongest external reference for confidence in full-canvas composition and direct manipulation.

Source: https://initiatearchitecture.com/

### pen.dev

Presents an agentic design canvas where code and design move both directions. The product supports import, editable layers, SVG paths, shaders, code-on-canvas and HTML/CSS export.

Application to V22: supports the process decision that the composition should be treated as a first-class visual artifact rather than a side-effect of production DOM styling.

Sources:
- https://www.pen.dev/
- https://docs.pen.dev/design-and-code/design-to-code
- https://docs.pen.dev/core-concepts/code-on-canvas

### EigenPal

Frames document workflows around human checkpoints, logged steps, reversibility, permission-aware search and evaluation on historical data.

Application to V22: trust/evidence/human review should be visible in the work surface rather than isolated in a governance appendix.

Source: https://www.eigenpal.com/

### Cohere North

Uses large product surfaces, whitespace and capability groupings to explain an enterprise AI workspace.

Application to V22: one convincing work surface can carry more explanatory weight than many small explanatory cards.

Source: https://cohere.com/north

### Invisible Technologies

Its public 'How we work' narrative moves from systems connection to operational modelling, validation and measurement.

Application to V22: evidence and validation can be part of the operational story rather than a detached control layer.

Source: https://invisibletech.ai/how-we-work

### Lokalise

The project editor keeps keys, translations, screenshots, comments and metadata in one contextual workspace; screenshots can be linked directly to the items they contextualise.

Application to V22: source, interpretation, object and context should often remain simultaneously inspectable on a 1920-wide workspace.

Sources:
- https://docs.lokalise.com/en/articles/2089277-project-editor
- https://docs.lokalise.com/en/articles/2045882-screenshots

### Atticus

Combines readable editorial communication with explicit scope/boundary statements on a high-stakes subject.

Application to V22: qualifiers such as proposed, historical, missing, not observed and not established can be primary design elements rather than fine print.

Sources:
- https://www.atticus.com/
- https://www.atticus.com/disclaimer

### Weave Bio

Describes an AI-native regulatory knowledge lifecycle while keeping human expert scrutiny central.

Application to V22: useful analogue for high-stakes knowledge work where AI assists but human expertise and review remain authoritative.

Source: https://www.weave.bio/about/

## Synthesis

The references support three coexisting modes rather than one uniform UI density:

1. **Editorial / explanatory** — orientation and system understanding.
2. **Spatial / interactive** — relationships, transformations, impact and conditions.
3. **Analytical workspace** — source, object, search, evidence and comparison.

V22's earlier runtime leaned too heavily toward mode 3 with limited decoration from mode 1. The composition reset should prove that all three can coexist in one identity before production propagation.

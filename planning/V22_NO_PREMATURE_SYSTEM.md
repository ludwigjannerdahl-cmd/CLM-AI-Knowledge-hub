# No Premature System

Do not create or expand a V22 design system before the accepted golden compositions exist.

The first V22 styling attempt showed the risk of system-first work: coherent tokens and components can still converge on generic product UI.

During the composition sprint:

- no new global token taxonomy unless a prototype requires it locally;
- no component inventory as a deliverable;
- no requirement that the three scenes share one layout;
- no propagation to production surfaces;
- no generic visual 'coverage' work.

After acceptance, extract only the grammar that is visible in the successful compositions.

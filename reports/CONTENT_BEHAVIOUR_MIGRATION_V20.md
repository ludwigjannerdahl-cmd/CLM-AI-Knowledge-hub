## Important boundaries

- No current approval, production, measured benefit, interface, named owner or legal classification is invented.
- The independent handoff case is synthetic. Its source is not an AMLR provision.
- A document does not become an executable skill through storage or formatting.
- The operating plane remains implementation/control/evidence, distinct from cloud runtime.
- Strategy horizons run concurrently; platform lanes do not grant workload permission.
- Authoritative action, present-task acceptance and reuse review remain different questions.
- Old evidence retains its actual scope. No test result or version history is fabricated.
- Local records and notes remain editable browser data, not institutional records.
- Private Mentor material, captured personal classifier history, analytics and original source-page scripts remain excluded from distribution.

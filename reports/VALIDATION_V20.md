## Required browser and reader acceptance before merge

1. Compare overview, knowledge, condition routes, search, dossier, source reader, relationship context/list, change, evidence, topic reading and scoping against the reference deck at the requested widths and zoom. Reject label collision, tiny essential text, clipping, confusing selection or a visibly weaker composition.
2. Read the opening without clicking. Ask an unfamiliar colleague what work AI assists, how context supports it and where to start. Do not coach them. Record their words, errors and time; revise failed orientation.
3. Follow preparation through source, subject, output and review. Ask what is supported, missing and reserved for human judgement or authorised action. Change subject and inspect the applicability consequence.
4. Follow development and distinguish its immediate value from reuse and shared production. A deterministic output must not be understood as requiring a production AI runtime.
5. Toggle interface, access, context consumption and runtime separately. Confirm the relevant path changes in place. Explain why supplied-material work can continue, why runtime does not create an interface, and why no switch authorises a record update.
6. Explore both change cases. Confirm independent source identities and pinned evidence scope. An old or empty evidence record must never become a pass for the proposed revision.
7. Open a deep search result, object panel and source passage. Exercise Back/Forward, close/Escape, return, theme, presentation mode, notes and exports. Confirm all selections, relevant disclosures, focus and scroll survive where intended.
8. Exercise scoping blank → valid → amendment → input edit; JSON and review-brief exports; replay; unreadable/old record retention; storage denial; and file metadata. No false approval, silent loss or stale result may appear.
9. Ask the reader to apply the model to a new task, propose a bounded next decision and explain it to another colleague. The model fails if they repeat labels but need Ludwig to reconstruct the reasoning.

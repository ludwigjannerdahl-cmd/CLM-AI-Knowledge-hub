# Useful work first — implementation and visual review

16 October 2026. Isolated design study; no production propagation.

## What changed

The previous abstractions were rejected as insufficiently informative. This pass builds a complete synthetic CLM preparation example from the canonical objects in `src/data.js`. It is one representative task, not a new source-of-wealth ontology for the whole Hub.

The statement “I sold my company in 2024” is visible beside the instruction, reported FenX field meaning, preparation method, candidate observation, human review, requirement, proposed test and evidence record. Exact source limitations, versions, subject distinctions, mappings, failure modes and provenance are inspectable without moving into another application.

## Actual comparison and rejection

The supplied Design Board 8 and Design System 15 were inspected alongside the actual Chromium renders. Their useful qualities are material distinction, authored hierarchy, simultaneous context, and control/evidence at the point of work. Generated claims, approvals, metrics and layouts were not copied. Private reference boards are not distributed in this repository.

| Architecture | What the geometry helps with | Observed failure | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: preparation workbench | Source and output compare across the canvas; human review is a separate margin | Source plane takes too much height; requirement/test extend below 1200px | Reject as delivered; basis for structural revision |
| B: annotated case folio | Instruction and method annotate a dominant prepared output | Oversized output and narrow source distract from the complete mechanism; lower detail clipped | Reject |
| C: trace ledger | Compares statement, field meaning and absence by proposition | Review/evidence arrive below the first frame; too much reading before seeing accountability | Reject as the opening; comparison pattern may remain useful later |
| Revised workbench | Four simultaneous artifact planes; separate development/evidence bench; selected facts traced across actual fragments | First revision still clipped the evidence link; header height was reduced without reducing core text | Selected candidate for this bounded scene |

The rejected initial directions also used a misleading linear label, “Source constrained by Instruction”. The corrected direction uses a reading sequence and places the instruction beside preparation: it does not claim that the instruction changes the source.

## Material revision

- Source and instruction occupy one material column; field meaning and method occupy another.
- Candidate output retains a distinct deep-green surface. Human review is separated by a review boundary, not presented as an AI step.
- Requirement, adverse test and missing evidence are visible together beneath the task.
- The long introduction was shortened to recover space for evidence rather than shrink its type.
- Trace selection highlights the actual event, field mapping, or missing amount. Missing-amount tracing joins source absence, unresolved output, acceptance logic and the proposed test.
- Removing field context withholds the candidate mapping. It does not invent a field, category, interface or permission.
- The test contrasts a guessed value with the expected unresolved value. Its label says **proposed**. It is not a recorded execution.

## Independent gates

**Visual quality — selected after rendered review, not production acceptance.** The revision uses the 1920×1200 canvas for simultaneous context. Its visual distinction comes from source paper, an unboxed field structure, bounded output, a human review margin and an evidence bench. All core artifacts fit the first frame. The initial three directions do not pass. Whether this reaches Ludwig's exceptional-quality bar remains a design judgement for review; technical success does not establish that judgement.

**Semantic correctness — passes the bounded specimen review.** Canonical object/source snapshots match `src/data.js`. Source-reported field knowledge is not described as current operational data. No categorical lookup, amount, verification, approval, production integration, measured benefit or observed test result is invented. Task acceptance, reuse review and evidence remain distinct. No legal claim has been newly derived. A complete enterprise domain review is not claimed.

**Informational value — concrete reasoning is inspectable; independent comprehension remains untested.** Without opening details, the reader can identify the source statement, instruction, subject/field meaning, method, proposed result, unresolved facts, review responsibility, requirement, adverse case and absent evidence. Inspection supplies exact object scope and source limitations. Removing context and tracing absence demonstrate a consequence, not just a new caption. No unfamiliar colleague or second-hand retell test has been conducted.

No averaging of these gates. The visual ambition and independent reader-comprehension questions remain open. Other stories and production propagation remain on hold.

## Interaction and evidence boundaries

- Native dialog, Escape, focus return, related-object inspection and browser history.
- Trace, context assumption and demonstration-review selection are URL state and survive reload/history.
- The source reader exposes only the single supplied synthetic line; it does not fabricate a longer transcript.
- The reported extraction schema is described as reported. Review/reuse annotations are not passed off as runtime output fields.
- Test-run evidence for this website does **not** fill `EVID-01@1`. That object still has no implementation version, execution or observed result.
- No storage or institutional audit log, customer write, live model, enterprise integration or access change is introduced.

## Preservation and limits

`src/`, `docs/` and `sources/` are unchanged from the V22 composition-reset baseline. Original Hub content, tools, navigation and behaviour are retained in production; this isolated scene does not claim to replace or integrate them yet.

Approved SEB Sans Serif binaries are absent. Local font names are requested, but CI renders the explicit system fallback; the browser confirms the SEB face is unavailable. No font has been fabricated.

The ordinary page scrolls. Mobile presents the same artifacts in a readable sequence instead of scaling the desktop composition. Mobile and reduced-motion checks support compatibility, not a completed whole-Hub accessibility audit.
